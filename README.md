# nyc-standup

**Open mics for people trying standup for the first time.** Short list, hand-verified, dated.

Most open-mic directories try to list everything and quietly go stale. This site does the
opposite: a deliberately small list of NYC-area mics that are genuinely doable for a
first-timer, with the fields that actually matter when you've never done this before —
how sign-up works, what it costs, how long your set is, whether you need to bring
an audience (you never do, here), and **when a human last verified the entry**.

Plus a no-fluff guide: [Your First Open Mic](guide.html) — the light, the etiquette,
the four sign-up systems, and a step-by-step first night.

## Honesty policy

- Every entry carries a `last_verified` date and a method (`web` / `phone-only` / `in-person`).
- Schedules in this city change weekly. Entries we couldn't fully confirm are marked
  **call ahead** instead of pretending.
- This site is a curated complement to [badslava](https://badslava.com/new-york-open-mics.php)
  and [openmicx](https://openmicx.com/new-york), not a replacement. Want everything? Go there.
  Want your first mic this week? Start here.

## Corrections & contributions

Spotted a dead mic, a changed time, or a beginner-friendly room we're missing?
[Open an issue](../../issues) or email **nycstandup.site@gmail.com**. Please include a
source (link, or "I was there on <date>"). Reports without evidence go into a
to-verify queue rather than straight into the data.

**Venue owners / mic producers:** want your listing corrected or removed?
Email **nycstandup.site@gmail.com** or open an issue — takedown on request,
no questions asked.

## Tech

No framework, no build step. `data.js` (the mic data) + two hand-written HTML pages.
Deployed on Vercel; every entry is reviewed by a human before merge.
